Flood Risk & Disaster Response System

Household flood-risk prediction, incident reporting and flood-aware rescue routing for the municipality's 26 barangays.

Report a flood or emergencyFor residents. Send your location, water depth and how many people need help. You get a reference number to follow up. MDRRMO operations consoleFor staff. Registry of residents and their predicted risk, incident queue, evacuation centres and the safest rescue routes. Household self-assessmentCheck one location's flood hazard data and its predicted risk across 60 historical rain events. Nothing is saved. GIS explorer2-D / 3-D map of terrain, roads, buildings and source rasters used by the system.
In a life-threatening emergency call 911 or the MDRRMO hotline first. Risk levels come from a model trained on rule-based labels (Project NOAH, MGB, terrain and NASA POWER rain); they support, and do not replace, the judgement of responders and official PAGASA/NDRRMC warnings.
