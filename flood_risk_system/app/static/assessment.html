    Sources and interpretation

    Elevation and slope come from Copernicus DEM, distance to waterway from OpenStreetMap, land cover from ESA WorldCover 2021, and past surface water from JRC Global Surface Water (1984–2024), all read at the selected point. Flood hazard comes from Project NOAH maps (5-, 25- and 100-year rain): the barangay class is the highest NOAH class covering at least 25% of the barangay; the house class is the NOAH zone at the point. MGB flood susceptibility (Mines and Geosciences Bureau) is read at the point; it also marks flood-prone areas that NOAH’s rain simulations leave out. NOAH “None” and MGB “NotMapped” mean outside the mapped flood areas, not that the place cannot flood.

    Riverside: under 100 m; NearWaterway: 100–300 m; Inland: 300 m or more. Missing source values remain unavailable. Rainfall is not chosen by date: the prediction runs the household through 60 historical NASA POWER rain events (1991–2025), and static map layers paired with past rainfall do not reconstruct past household conditions.
