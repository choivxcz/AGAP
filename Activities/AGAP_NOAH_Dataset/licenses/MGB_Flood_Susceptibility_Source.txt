MGB Detailed Flood Susceptibility - source and terms
====================================================

Source: Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB), Geospatial Data Inventory, "Detailed Flood Susceptibility"
https://controlmap.mgb.gov.ph/arcgis/rest/services/GeospatialDataInventory/GDI_Detailed_Flood_Susceptibility/MapServer/0
Downloaded October 25, 2026. Re-checked November 5, 2026: the service was online (Map, Query, Data enabled), used the
same field (FloodSusc) and codes (MF, HF, VHF), and returned HF at a test point in Santisima Cruz, matching the file.

Used for: the MGBFloodSusceptibility column and, through it, the flood hazard in the RiskLevel rule.

License: the service states no license, copyright text or terms of use. It is public government data. Credit
"Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB)". Confirm reuse with MGB before publishing a dataset that contains it.

Meaning of the classes, from MGB's description on its public GeoRisk service
(https://ulap-hazards.georisk.gov.ph/arcgis/rest/services/MGBPublic/Flood/MapServer):
Moderate = flood heights above 0.5 m up to 1 m, 1-3 days; High = above 1 m up to 2 m, more than 3 days;
Very High = above 2 m, more than 3 days.

The source files and the service's own layer description are in C:\Datasets_Pref\MGB_Flood_Susceptibility\.
