--- How it applies to this dataset ---

Used for: the Barangay column, the check that each pin is inside its barangay, and the barangay
areas used to compute FloodHazard5yr/25yr/100yr.
This is a proprietary license, not an open one. It allows internal use and printed maps that credit
GEODATA Systems Technologies, Inc., but it does not allow the data or products derived from it to be
distributed in digital form to third parties. It also cannot be combined with the ODbL share-alike
requirement for a public release.
Recommendation: acceptable for internal thesis work with credit. Replace these boundaries with an
openly licensed set (for example PSA or NAMRIA boundaries, after confirming their license) before
the dataset or the system is shared publicly.
